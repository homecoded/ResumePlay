ResumePlay

Paste a YouTube video or playlist. ResumePlay keeps your place in it, even if you close the tab.

Play through YouTube?

ResumePlay uses YouTube's player. To load it, your browser contacts Google: Google sees your IP address and what you watch, sets its own cookies, and may transfer this data to the USA. ResumePlay itself does no tracking.
